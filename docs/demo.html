Generalized State-of-Health Estimation from Partial Charging Curves with Transfer Learning across Battery Chemistries

Lina Chen, Ming Zhao, Yuki Sato · Journal of Energy Storage · 2024

Accurate state-of-health (SOH) estimation is essential for safe and reliable operation of lithium-ion batteries. This work introduces a generalized data-driven approach that estimates SOH from partial charging curves without requiring complete cycles.

3. Methodology

We train a Bayesian hyperparameter-tuned neural network on 16 features extracted from partial charging segments. Transfer learning is then applied across three battery chemistries to improve generalization.

图 2 · 不同充电窗口长度下的 MAPE（本图为模拟绘制）

4. Results

The proposed model achieves a mean absolute percentage error of 1.43% across the evaluated chemistries, outperforming the baseline on unseen partial charging windows.

这篇论文的核心贡献和局限性分别是什么？
核心贡献是从部分充电曲线估计 SOH，并用迁移学习覆盖多种化学体系，报告 MAPE 约为 1.43% [第 1 页]。局限性在于：实验仅覆盖三种化学体系，且对充电片段长度与数据质量较敏感 [第 8 页]。
  • 贡献：无需完整循环，适合在线估计
  • 方法：贝叶斯调参 + 迁移学习
  • 风险：跨体系泛化仍需更多验证
