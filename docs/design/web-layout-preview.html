Generalized State-of-Health Estimation from Partial Charging Curves with Transfer Learning
3 / 5
Journal of Energy Storage · 2024Chen et al. · 3

5 Results

Abstract. We evaluate whether a transferable health estimator can remain accurate when only a short charging window is available. The proposed schedule is compared with an equivalent-circuit baseline, a temporal convolution model, and a physics-informed network across three chemistries.

Table 1 summarizes the main comparison. The proposed model reduces mean absolute percentage error by 0.31 percentage points over the strongest baseline and lowers the worst-case error on short segments from 4.8% to 3.2%.

Gains are consistent across the three chemistries, although LFP remains the most difficult target. The voltage plateau of LFP is comparatively flat, so short-window slope and incremental capacity features are less separable.

Figure 1 shows that the advantage is largest when the charging window is short. With ten minutes of data, the baseline degrades sharply, whereas the proposed model retains a usable estimate.
